One line of quiet, earned pride: a boy who stuttered reads his poem to the last line in front of his class, after a year of reading to his dog each night. The program writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` — "After a year of reading to his dog each night, the boy who stuttered read his poem to the last line, and no one in class looked away." (133 characters). Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "One Line of Earned Pride" bounty, $5 in USDC.

## Run it

mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
